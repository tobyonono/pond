# Reference source logos

These marks identify the connected source and link to its platform. They remain
their owners' trademarks; they are not part of Pond's original MIT glyph artwork.

- **Goodreads:** the g outline and its counter come from the current first-party
  `GoodreadsWordmark` SVG at https://www.goodreads.com/. The tile uses its official
  favicon colors (`#EBE2D7`, `#1E1915`), with optical sizing for Pond's square tiles.
  The mark was introduced in the [official logo announcement](https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/2984-introducing-our-new-logo-and-more-ways-we-continue-to-improve-goodreads).
- **Letterboxd:** the colored dots, overlap paths and colors come from the
  [official dots decal SVG](https://a.ltrbxd.com/logos/letterboxd-decal-dots-pos-rgb.svg),
  linked from https://letterboxd.com/about/brand/. The circular background is
  extended to a square to match Pond's existing brand tiles. Mask IDs were removed
  by retaining the fully covered ellipse edges and the original overlap paths, so
  multiple icons in the same document cannot collide.

The components bundle the marks locally and make no logo requests at runtime.
